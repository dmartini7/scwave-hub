Coaching Philosophy

I believe in developing complete players — athletes who are technically sound, tactically intelligent, physically prepared, and mentally strong. My goal is not just to help you win games this season, but to build habits and a love for the game that will serve you throughout your life and career.

At SC Wave, we compete at the MLS NEXT level — the highest youth development platform in the country. That demands professionalism, commitment, and a growth mindset every single day. I will always challenge you to be better, communicate openly, and hold yourself to the standards this program requires.

My Background

I have been coaching competitive youth soccer at the highest levels for over 10 years. In addition to my role as MLS NEXT Director at SC Wave, I am also the Owner & Executive Director of the Illinois Futsal Factory Academy — one of the premier futsal development programs in the Midwest. I hold advanced coaching licenses and have developed players who have gone on to compete at the college and professional levels.

📲 How to Reach Me

All team communication goes through PlayMetrics. If you need to report a late arrival or absence, message me through PlayMetrics as early as possible. For a personal conversation about your development, reach out to request a time — I am always open to an honest dialogue.

For Parents: Please follow the 24-hour rule — wait 24 hours after any game or event before reaching out about a coaching decision. If your player has a concern, encourage them to talk to me first. That builds the communication skills they need at this level.

🎯 How to Use This Hub

This training hub is designed for you to use between sessions — during the season and especially during the winter. Use the Tactical & Mental section to study the game. Use Technical to train skills at home or on your own. Use Physical to stay sharp. The more you invest here, the more you'll see on the field.
