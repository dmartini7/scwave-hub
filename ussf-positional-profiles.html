USSF Talent Identification Philosophy

Talent is developed in the "right" environment — culture, coaching, and context all matter.

Talent identification starts with the clubs, who need quality, diverse, and expansive scouting networks.

Identification must happen in the context of the game, accounting for conditions, level, and demands.

Players develop at different rates — performance trajectories are non-linear. Don't peak too early.

Talent ID is a long-term process where current performance and future potential are influenced by environment, age, and maturation.

Key qualities differ by club and federation — they must connect to identity: core values, style of play, and culture.

Scouts use a common structure and language for examining players across the system.

Framework is gender-neutral with gender-specific scouting strategy, built on relationships with all communities.

Key Qualities of a Player
🧩 Game Understanding & Decision-Making
  • Reads situations, anticipates, and makes quick decisions under pressure
  • Identifies cues and intentions of teammates and opponents
  • Recognizes space, time, and risk opportunities
  • Processes information quickly — thinks ahead, acts fast
  • Manages pace of game; knows when to accelerate or slow down
  • Understands role based on style of play, team principles, and strategy
  • Scans continuously; adapts body shape
  • Versatile — can play multiple roles in different systems
  • Uses creativity to find new solutions
🎯 Responsibility & Initiative
  • Demonstrates self-knowledge and applies it to their role within the team
  • Takes ownership — sets goals, plans, monitors, re-evaluates
  • Takes initiative; sees opportunities without being told
  • Manages personal lifestyle (rest, recovery, sleep, nutrition, mental health)
  • Behaves like a role model under all circumstances
  • Prepares for every game and session
  • Reflects on and evaluates own performance (self-critical)
  • Receives feedback, asks questions — coachable
  • Creates a personal development plan; holds self accountable
🔥 Growth Mindset & Winning Mentality
  • Intensity: Gives maximum effort; concentrates; commits to the process
  • Communication: Communicates with teammates on and off the field to solve situations and improve
  • Shows joy, passion, and enthusiasm — fun and freedom
  • Challenges self; pursues goals; never gives up
  • Faces adversity and overcomes fear — brave
  • Pushes self to limits (body and mind)
  • Believes in self and contributes deliberately to team performance
  • Emotional Stability: Overcomes setbacks, deals with pressure, stays composed, behaves maturely
⚽ Technical Execution
  • Moving easily with the ball, staying calm under pressure
  • Receiving and playing in tight spaces — use of both feet
  • Receiving to play forward; playing 1 and 2 touch
  • Precise passing that sets up the receiver (short, long, break lines)
  • Dribbling and running with the ball to break lines and accelerate attack
  • Making quick combinations; creating chances and finishing
  • Heading; dominating 1v1 duels
💪 Physical Execution
  • Moves efficiently — agility, coordination, balance
  • Uses body in duels to protect ball, control, or disrupt opponent
  • Makes quick, explosive movements to separate from opponent
  • Repeats high-intensity actions (sprints)
  • Exudes strength, power, and endurance
  • Has specialized physical attributes for positional role
📈 Learning Ability
  • Shows an ability to excel at a higher level
  • Adapts to new situations quickly
  • Absorbs and applies new information quickly
  • Observed over an extended time — includes both games and training sessions
SC Wave Positional Profiles
#1

Goalkeeper

GK · Last line of defense, first line of attack
Key Qualities
Confident Athletic Decisive Coach on the Field Accurate Passing (Short & Long)
Player Actions
PhaseKey Actions
Attacking Position to support for back pass; play forward or wide to begin build-up; push up with the team; disorganize opponent with variety and confidence in distribution
Defending Command the box; secure hands when catching; aggression and confidence in 1v1; read opponent's body shape; organize teammates in front; position to cover long balls behind defensive line
Transition D→A Pass forward (long) quickly to exploit the opponent and create 1v1; keep the ball when cannot play forward; push the defensive line up
Transition A→D Stay connected to the back line; organize teammates — balanced and compact; position behind defensive line to take preventative action
Physical: Strength & power, agility, quick reactions — control the area on air and ground
#4/5

Center Backs

CB · Defensive backbone and build-out starters
Key Qualities
Positionally Dominant Vocal Leader Technically Skilled Athletic Aerially Dominant
Player Actions
PhaseKey Actions
Attacking Position to spread out and provide passing option for GK; make accurate forward/diagonal passes between or behind opposition lines; move forward with the ball to create 2v1; push defensive line and organize teammates
Defending Deny opposition in 1v1 situations (air & ground); be first to the ball on crosses; lead & organize defensive line; intercept long & through passes; movements in partnership with the other center back
Transition D→A Move quickly into position to support in space; pass or dribble forward; coach defensive line to push up as a unit
Transition A→D If closest to the situation, pressure the ball immediately to prevent danger; quick decision to pressure, delay, or drop back; take preventive defensive action
Physical: Strength & power in duels (jumping), speed and agility
#2/3

Full Backs

FB · Wide defensive balance and attacking threat
Key Qualities
High Endurance Proficient Dribbler High Concentration & Anticipation Proficient Cross & Long Passing Strong Defending 1v1
Player Actions
PhaseKey Actions
Attacking Position wide and high to receive and create space; support with runs behind; overlapping and underlapping runs to create 1v1 or 2v1; play early cross when space is behind the defensive line
Defending Pressure the ball to regain or delay the attack; deny penetration and prevent crosses in 1v1; move centrally to provide cover when ball is on opposite side; reposition to intercept passes
Transition D→A Give quick support in space (forward and wide); move up with defensive line as a collective unit; pass or dribble forward quickly to exploit disorganized opponent
Transition A→D If closest to situation, pressure ball immediately; if farthest, drop back quickly to re-position with defensive line; make decision to pressure or drop based on situation
Physical: Speed, quickness, agility and endurance
#6

Defensive Midfielder

DCM · Engine room, shield, and tempo-setter
Key Qualities
Strong Tackling Athletic Positionally Dominant Accurate & Efficient Distribution Controls & Dictates Tempo
Player Actions
PhaseKey Actions
Attacking Position centrally in spaces between lines to receive and play forward; make diagonal passes to unbalance the opponent; align movements with other central midfielders; dribble forward when space is open; support attack with forward runs
Defending Defend central space in front of center backs; pressure the ball and help teammates outnumber opponent; organize teammates to stay compact; deny penetration in 1v1
Transition D→A Move into space to support wide and forward; make diagonal passes or dribble to create 2v1; manage pace of game when cannot play forward
Transition A→D Pressure ball immediately if closest to situation; quick decision to pressure, delay, or drop back; position to take preventive defensive action
Physical: Agility, endurance, quickness, and use of body to protect the ball
#8

Central Midfielder

CM · Box-to-box engine and link between lines
Key Qualities
High Endurance Proficient Dribbler Calm Under Pressure Positionally Dominant Accurate & Efficient Passing Accurate Finishing 18–30 yds
Player Actions
PhaseKey Actions
Attacking Position between lines to receive and play forward; align movements with other midfielders and triangulate; dribble forward when space is open; support attack with forward runs behind the defensive line; position to support in the box
Defending Prevent opponent from dribbling or passing forward; recover to defensive third when needed; organize and coach teammates to press
Transition D→A Move into space quickly; make diagonal passes forward or dribble to create 2v1; manage game pace when cannot play forward; position to exploit opponent and create chances
Transition A→D Pressure ball immediately if closest; quick decision to pressure, delay, or drop back
#10

Attacking Central Midfielder

ACM · Creative force, final-third threat
Key Qualities
Dynamic Dribbler Creative Calm Under Pressure Positionally Dominant Accurate & Dynamic Passing Accurate Finishing (All Ranges) Penetrates Back Line — With & Without Ball
Player Actions
PhaseKey Actions
Attacking Occupy half space; receive between lines and play forward; penetrate through dribble or pass; create overloads; dynamic runs into the box; shooting from outside the box
Defending Organize teammates to press; decide to pressure ball, stay zonal, or track opposition holding midfielder; prevent forward passing or dribbling
Transition D→A Position and support quickly in space to exploit opponent and create chances
Transition A→D Pressure ball immediately if closest; quick decision to pressure, delay, or drop back
#7/11

Wide Forwards

WF · 1v1 creators, wide and central threat
Key Qualities
Dynamic Dribbler (1v1) Creative Positionally Dominant Accurate & Dynamic Crossing High Endurance & Good Speed Accurate Finishing (Inside & Outside Box)
Player Actions
PhaseKey Actions
Attacking Position high and wide to spread opposition; vary supporting movements in space between/behind defensive lines; take on defenders 1v1 — outside to cross or inside to score; move into goal-scoring positions in the box or at back post
Defending Recover and track into defensive third to assist fullback; move centrally for cover when ball is on opposite side; monitor situation to press in identified moments; prevent opponent from dribbling or passing forward in 1v1
Transition D→A Move quickly into position in space; position and support to exploit opponent and create chances
Transition A→D Pressure ball immediately if closest; quick decision to pressure, delay, or drop — follow direction from teammates behind
Physical: Speed, change of direction, body feints, repeated accelerations
#9

Center Forward

FWD · Finisher, target man, and pressure starter
Key Qualities
Dynamic Dribbler Creative Good Touch & Control Positionally Dominant Aerially Dominant Athletic Accurate Finishing (Inside & Outside Box)
Player Actions
PhaseKey Actions
Attacking Position high and centrally as a target to occupy opposition CBs; receive, hold/protect, and lay off the ball; make varied forward runs behind the defensive line; pass or dribble forward for 1v1 or 2v1; unmark with well-timed runs in the box to finish or create for teammates
Defending Force the opponent to play to one side; prevent switching the field; pressure the ball and help teammates outnumber opponent; press in identified situations; track opposition center back when needed
Transition D→A Move quickly onside and into space high up the field; hold and protect ball while teammates support the attack
Transition A→D Position for clearances; prevent opponent from sustaining attack by playing backward; pressure ball immediately if closest
Physical: Strength to hold off opponent, explosiveness to separate
