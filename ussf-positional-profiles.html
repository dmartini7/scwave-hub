Talent ID Philosophy
USSF
★

Talent is developed in the right environment — culture, coaching, and context all matter.

★

Talent ID starts with clubs, who need quality, diverse, and expansive scouting networks.

★

Identification must happen in the context of the game — accounting for conditions, level, and demands.

★

Players develop at different rates — performance trajectories are non-linear. Don't peak too early.

★

Key qualities must connect to the club's identity: core values, style of play, and culture.

★

Scouts use a common structure and language for examining players across the entire system.

★

Gender-neutral framework with gender-specific scouting strategy, built on community relationships.

Key Qualities of a Player
6 PILLARS
🧩 Game Understanding & Decision-Making
  • Reads situations, anticipates, and makes quick decisions under pressure
  • Identifies cues and intentions of teammates and opponents
  • Recognizes space, time, and risk opportunities
  • Processes information quickly — thinks ahead, acts fast
  • Manages pace of game; knows when to accelerate or slow down
  • Understands role based on style of play, principles, and strategy
  • Scans continuously; adapts body shape; versatile across systems
  • Uses creativity to find new solutions
🎯 Responsibility & Initiative
  • Demonstrates self-knowledge and applies it to their role
  • Takes ownership — sets goals, plans, monitors, re-evaluates
  • Takes initiative; sees opportunities without being told
  • Manages personal lifestyle (rest, recovery, sleep, nutrition, mental health)
  • Behaves like a role model under all circumstances
  • Reflects and evaluates own performance — self-critical
  • Receives feedback, asks questions — coachable
  • Holds self accountable; creates a personal development plan
🔥 Growth Mindset & Winning Mentality
  • Intensity: Maximum effort, full concentration, committed to the process
  • Communication: On and off the field — solves situations and improves self and team
  • Shows joy, passion, and enthusiasm — fun and freedom
  • Challenges self; pursues goals; never gives up
  • Faces adversity and overcomes fear — brave
  • Pushes self to limits — body and mind
  • Emotional Stability: Overcomes setbacks, stays composed under pressure, behaves maturely
⚽ Technical Execution
  • Moves easily with the ball; stays calm under pressure
  • Receives and plays in tight spaces — both feet
  • Receives to play forward; plays 1 and 2 touch
  • Precise passing that sets up the receiver (short, long, break lines)
  • Dribbling and running to break lines and accelerate attack
  • Making quick combinations; creating chances and finishing
  • Heading; dominating 1v1 duels
💪 Physical Execution
  • Moves efficiently — agility, coordination, balance
  • Uses body in duels to protect ball or disrupt opponent
  • Quick, explosive movements to separate from opponent
  • Repeats high-intensity actions (sprints)
  • Exudes strength, power, and endurance
  • Specialized physical attributes for positional role
📈 Learning Ability
  • Shows an ability to excel at a higher level
  • Adapts to new situations quickly
  • Absorbs and applies new information quickly
  • Observed over extended time — includes games and training sessions

#1

Goalkeeper

GK · Last line of defense, first line of attack
Key Qualities
Confident Athletic Decisive Coach on the Field Accurate Passing (Short & Long)
Player Actions by Phase
PhaseKey Actions
⚡ Attacking Support for back pass; play forward or wide to begin build-up; push up with the team; disorganize opponent with variety and confidence in distribution
🛡 Defending Command the box; secure hands when catching; aggression and confidence in 1v1; read opponent's body shape; organize teammates; cover long balls behind defensive line
↗ Trans D→A Pass forward (long) quickly to exploit and create 1v1; keep the ball when cannot play forward; push the defensive line up
↙ Trans A→D Stay connected to the back line; organize teammates — balanced and compact; position behind defensive line to take preventative action
⚡ Strength & power, agility, quick reactions — dominate the area on air and ground
#4/5

Center Backs

CB · Defensive backbone and build-out starters
Key Qualities
Positionally Dominant Vocal Leader Technically Skilled Athletic Aerially Dominant
Player Actions by Phase
PhaseKey Actions
⚡ Attacking Spread out and provide passing option for GK; make accurate forward/diagonal passes between or behind opposition lines; move forward to create 2v1; push defensive line and organize teammates
🛡 Defending Deny opposition in 1v1 (air & ground); first to the ball on crosses; lead & organize defensive line; intercept long & through passes; coordinate movements with partner center back
↗ Trans D→A Move quickly into space; pass or dribble forward; coach defensive line to push up as a unit
↙ Trans A→D If closest to situation, pressure ball immediately; quick decision to pressure, delay, or drop; take preventive defensive action
⚡ Strength & power in duels (jumping), speed and agility
#2/3

Full Backs

FB · Wide defensive balance and attacking threat
Key Qualities
High Endurance Proficient Dribbler High Concentration & Anticipation Proficient Cross & Long Passing Strong 1v1 Defending
Player Actions by Phase
PhaseKey Actions
⚡ Attacking Position wide and high to receive and create space; overlapping and underlapping runs to create 1v1 or 2v1; play early cross when space is behind defensive line
🛡 Defending Pressure ball to regain or delay attack; deny penetration and prevent crosses in 1v1; move centrally for cover when ball is on opposite side
↗ Trans D→A Quick support in space (forward and wide); move up with defensive line as a unit; dribble or pass forward quickly to exploit disorganized opponent
↙ Trans A→D If closest, pressure ball immediately; if farthest, drop back to re-position with defensive line; decide based on situation
⚡ Speed, quickness, agility, and endurance
#6

Defensive Midfielder

DCM · Engine room, shield, and tempo-setter
Key Qualities
Strong Tackling Athletic Positionally Dominant Accurate & Efficient Distribution Controls & Dictates Tempo
Player Actions by Phase
PhaseKey Actions
⚡ Attacking Position centrally between lines to receive and play forward; diagonal passes to unbalance opponent; align movements with other midfielders; dribble forward when space is open; support attack with forward runs
🛡 Defending Defend central space in front of center backs; pressure ball and help teammates outnumber opponent; organize teammates to stay compact; deny penetration in 1v1
↗ Trans D→A Support wide and forward; diagonal passes or dribble to create 2v1; manage game pace when cannot play forward
↙ Trans A→D Pressure ball immediately if closest; quick decision to pressure, delay, or drop; position for preventive defensive action
⚡ Agility, endurance, quickness, and body to protect the ball
#8

Central Midfielder

CM · Box-to-box engine and link between lines
Key Qualities
High Endurance Proficient Dribbler Calm Under Pressure Positionally Dominant Accurate & Efficient Passing Accurate Finishing 18–30 yds
Player Actions by Phase
PhaseKey Actions
⚡ Attacking Position between lines; align movements and triangulate with other midfielders; dribble forward when space is open; support attack with runs behind defensive line; position in the box near goal
🛡 Defending Prevent opponent from dribbling or passing forward; recover to defensive third when needed; coach teammates to press in identified moments
↗ Trans D→A Move into space quickly; diagonal passes or dribble to create 2v1; manage game pace; position to exploit opponent and create chances
↙ Trans A→D Pressure ball immediately if closest; quick decision to pressure, delay, or drop back
#10

Attacking Central Midfielder

ACM · Creative force, final-third threat
Key Qualities
Dynamic Dribbler Creative Calm Under Pressure Positionally Dominant Accurate & Dynamic Passing Accurate Finishing (All Ranges) Penetrates Back Line — With & Without Ball
Player Actions by Phase
PhaseKey Actions
⚡ Attacking Occupy half space; receive between lines and play forward; penetrate through dribble or pass; create overloads; dynamic runs into the box; shoot from outside the box
🛡 Defending Organize teammates to press; decide to pressure ball, stay zonal, or track opposition holding midfielder; prevent forward passing or dribbling
↗ Trans D→A Position and support quickly in space to exploit opponent and create chances
↙ Trans A→D Pressure ball immediately if closest; quick decision to pressure, delay, or drop back
#7/11

Wide Forwards

WF · 1v1 creators, wide and central threat
Key Qualities
Dynamic Dribbler (1v1) Creative Positionally Dominant Accurate & Dynamic Crossing High Endurance & Good Speed Accurate Finishing (Inside & Outside Box)
Player Actions by Phase
PhaseKey Actions
⚡ Attacking Position high and wide to spread opposition; vary movements between/behind defensive lines; take on defenders 1v1 — outside to cross, inside to score; move into goal-scoring positions in the box or at back post
🛡 Defending Recover and track into defensive third to assist fullback; move centrally for cover when ball is opposite; press in identified moments; prevent opponent dribbling or passing forward in 1v1
↗ Trans D→A Move quickly into space; position and support to exploit opponent and create chances
↙ Trans A→D Pressure ball immediately if closest; quick decision to pressure, delay, or drop — follow direction from teammates behind
⚡ Speed, change of direction, body feints, and repeated accelerations
#9

Center Forward

FWD · Finisher, target man, and pressure starter
Key Qualities
Dynamic Dribbler Creative Good Touch & Control Positionally Dominant Aerially Dominant Athletic Accurate Finishing (Inside & Outside Box)
Player Actions by Phase
PhaseKey Actions
⚡ Attacking Position high and centrally as a target to occupy opposition CBs; receive, hold, protect, and lay off the ball; make varied forward runs behind defensive line; unmark with well-timed runs in the box to finish or create for teammates
🛡 Defending Force opponent to one side; prevent switching the field; pressure ball and help outnumber opponent; press in identified situations; track opposition center back when needed
↗ Trans D→A Move quickly onside and into space high up the field; hold and protect ball while teammates join the attack
↙ Trans A→D Position for clearances; prevent opponent from sustaining attack; pressure ball immediately if closest
⚡ Strength to hold off opponent, explosiveness to separate
