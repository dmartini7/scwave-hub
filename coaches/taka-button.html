TAKA → IDP in one click

Set this up once on your computer (Chrome, Safari or Edge). After that, any player's TAKA IDP fills their SC Wave IDP with one click.

1. Add the button (once)

  1. Show your bookmarks bar: press Cmd + Shift + B (Windows: Ctrl + Shift + B).
  2. Drag this yellow button up onto the bookmarks bar:
⚡ Send to SC Wave IDP ← drag it up to the bookmarks bar

2. Use it (every player)

  1. In TAKA, go to Assistant Coach → Individual Development Plan and choose the player.
  2. Click ⚡ Send to SC Wave IDP on your bookmarks bar. A small SC Wave panel appears on the TAKA page and reads the skills and every game from both seasons (about 10 seconds — don't click around while it works).
  3. When it says ✓ Got [player], click Fill SC Wave IDP →. The player's SC Wave IDP opens in a new tab, already filled:
    • TAKA skills (Key Strength / On Par / Development Opportunity) with each skill's score as a % of the Top 20% level
    • every game from Dec 2025 on — date, opponent, home/away, score
    • a suggested 1–4 game rating from TAKA's game score (change any you disagree with)
    • goals and jersey number
  4. Add your own four-corner ratings, strengths, goals and comments as usual.

If the player has no IDP yet, it is created for you from the roster. Running it again later updates the TAKA data and adds new games; ratings you changed by hand are kept.

Good to know

The button only reads the TAKA page you have open, using your own TAKA login. Nothing is sent anywhere until you click Fill SC Wave IDP, and then only to this coaches area. Paste from TAKA inside each IDP still works as a backup.
