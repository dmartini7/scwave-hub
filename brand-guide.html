Primary Mark & Clear Space
SC Wave Soccer Club  ·  Founded 1978  ·  MLS NEXT

Clear Space & Sizing

Always maintain clear space around the logo equal to the width of the "S" in the mark. Never crowd the logo with text or other graphic elements.

Minimum print size: 0.75 inches wide.
Minimum digital size: 60px wide.

Use on solid black or very dark backgrounds for maximum impact.

Use on white or light neutral backgrounds with full detail visible.

Do not place on busy photography or low-contrast colored backgrounds.

Do not distort, rotate, recolor, or add drop shadows to the logo.
