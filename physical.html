🏠 In-Home Workout Sessions

3 progressive workouts — each has a movement prep circuit and a 3-round strength circuit. No equipment needed.

Workout 1 — Circuit · 3 Rounds

Reverse Lunge to Knee Drive

25 each leg · drive the knee up explosively at the top

SL Glute Bridge (3 sec pause)

10 each leg · pause at the top, keep hips square

Burpees

10 reps · full extension at the top, chest to floor at the bottom

Wall Sit

1 minute · knees at 90°, back flat against the wall

Plank Ups

5 each side · alternate leading arm, keep hips level

Lunge Jumps

20 reps · explode off the back foot, switch legs in the air

Workout 2 — Circuit · 3 Rounds

Split Squat Jumps

10 each leg · land softly, stay balanced

Flutter Kicks

1 minute · lower back flat, small controlled kicks

Lunge Holds (knee hovering)

1 min each leg · back knee 1 inch off the floor, stay still

Skater Hops

20 reps · stick each landing for 1 second before jumping

Close Grip Push Ups

25 reps · hands shoulder-width, elbows stay tight to body

Side Star Plank Hold

30 sec each side · stack feet or stagger, raise top arm and leg

Glute Bridge March

20 each leg · hips stay elevated, slow controlled march

Push Up Position Bird Dogs

10 each side · extend opposite arm and leg, keep hips square

Workout 3 — Circuit · 3 Rounds

Single Leg Squat Jumps

15 each leg · opp knee drives to floor on landing, stay controlled

Copenhagen Side Plank w/ Knee Drive

10 each side · top foot on bench/couch, drive bottom knee up

Plyo Push Ups

15 reps · hands leave the floor at the top, land soft

Jump Tucks

10 reps · drive knees to chest, land and reset quickly

Plank Hold

1 minute · forearms down, back flat, squeeze glutes and abs

Mountain Climbers

30 each leg · hips low, fast alternating drives

Broad Jumps (stick landing)

10 reps · jump as far as possible, stick and hold each landing

Wall Sit

1 minute · finish the workout strong — no coming off the wall early

🏟️ At-Field Workouts & Speed Training

Field-based conditioning, speed development, and agility work. Study each exercise before heading out.

Death by 300s — Exercises

50 reps of each exercise — complete all 300 total reps as fast as possible

Broad Jumps

50 reps · max distance each jump, stick the landing

Push Up Ladder

50 reps · work through them in sets, rest as needed

Spiderman Crunches

50 reps · bring knee to elbow each rep, core stays tight

Burpees

50 reps · full extension at the top, pace yourself

Lunge Jumps

50 reps · alternate legs, stay explosive

Plyo Push Ups

50 reps · hands off the floor at the top each rep

Knee Hug Crunches

50 reps · controlled, full crunch hugging both knees in

Speed & Agility Drills

Lean Fall Run

2×10 yards each leg · punch out alternating legs, 2x each leg

Flying 30s

3 reps · cones at 15, 45, 60 yds — build to top speed through the 30

Pro Agility (5-10-5)

2x each direction · low start, explode and cut clean

Shuffle-Sprint

2x each way · lateral shuffle 10 yds, turn and sprint 10 yds

Outside X Drill

2x each way · 5×5 yard box, run the outside — both directions

Field Runs — Reference

Walking Lunges

Down and back the field · tall posture, long stride, no knee cave

Straight Leg Sit Ups

Legs flat, controlled sit-up — core strength for field conditioning
