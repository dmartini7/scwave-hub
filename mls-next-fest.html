MLS NEXT Fest 2026

December 2 – 13, 2026 · Arizona (Scottsdale & Mesa). Everything SC Wave Homegrown and Academy families need to know — dates, kickoff windows, and travel guidance.

✈️
Book travel carefully — MLS NEXT will not adjust the schedule for early flights. Kickoff times are not finalized until closer to the event, so flights must be booked around the potential first and last kickoff times below — not a guess at the actual match time.
Do not book an outbound flight assuming a late first kickoff, and do not book a return flight assuming an early finish on the last day. Clubs that book outside these windows are on their own — MLS NEXT has confirmed the schedule will not be adjusted to accommodate early travel.
📅
Event DatesDecember 2 – 13, 2026. Each age group plays on a specific window within that range — see the calendar below.
📍
LocationScottsdale (U13/U14) and Mesa, Arizona (U15–U19) — Arizona Athletic Grounds, Red Mountain, Scottsdale Sports Complex, Bell 94 & Reata.
🏨
Hotel BookingRequired through MLS NEXT's official travel partner, On Location — see details below.

Conduct & Banned Terms
Read before every match

"MLS NEXT prohibits discriminatory conduct and a red card will be issued to anyone who uses a Banned Term or makes a discriminatory gesture, and they will be suspended from future games. Yellow cards will be issued for unsporting behavior to any player who taunts the opposing team. Respect your opponent and the game, and let your play speak for itself."

— Read by the Match Official before every MLS NEXT Fest match

🚫
MLS NEXT is committed to facilitating a diverse and inclusive environment. Discrimination or offensive remarks of any kind arising out of or in connection with an individual's background, ethnicity, religious beliefs, sexual orientation, or identity, in English or any language, will not be tolerated.

If a Banned Term Is Used Mid-Match
  • 1️⃣
    The player tells the coach or referee they heard a banned term.
  • 2️⃣
    The referee stops the match and addresses both coaching staffs.
  • 3️⃣
    MLS NEXT staff come to the field to address both teams before play resumes.
⚠️
A confirmed Banned Term or discriminatory gesture results in a red card and at least a 3-match suspension. A team's 2nd act of discrimination or harassment during the Event can result in removal from the competition.

Spectator Conduct
For parents & family in the stands
  • ✅
    Cheer positively — respect referee decisions and avoid negative interactions with opposing players, coaches, or spectators.
  • 🪑
    Spectators must sit across from their own team's bench.
  • 🚭
    Alcohol, drugs, and tobacco are not permitted at venues.
  • ⚠️
    Spectators are subject to discipline, including removal from the Event, for violations of the MLS NEXT Code of Conduct.
